Collective settings by job size#

Reference — a description of the machinery. Look things up here.

The configurations that met Meta’s acceptance criteria on Nscale NC, by job size, and the signals used to tune them. Placement comes first; see rack placement and collective performance for why and how to place a multi-rack job for the procedure.

Measured 2026-10-24 to 2026-10-27 on GB300 NVL72 (4 GPUs per node), one ConnectX-8 per GPU split into 4 planes of 200 Gb/s, NVIDIA SN5610 leaves (Cumulus Linux 5.16.6), HPC-X 2.25.1, nccl-tests at 2 GB, 100 iterations after 50 warm-up, three rounds. On another platform or fabric these are starting points to re-measure, not answers.

NIC congestion-control profiles#

Spectrum-X ztr_rttcc parameters, register PPCC, written per ConnectX-8 (one PF per rail) with mlxreg -d <pci> -y --set cmd_type=8,value=<V> --reg_name PPCC --indexes local_port=1,pnat=0,lp_msb=0,algo_slot=0,algo_param_index=<idx> and read back with --get --op cmd_type=0x6. cmd_type=2 turns CC off. Never cmd_type=0x7: it zeroed a parameter on one NIC.
